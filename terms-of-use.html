Terms of use

A plain-language draft describing intended use of the DarkBlaze Group website.

Status: draft for owner review. These plain-language terms are not legal advice and have not been reviewed by counsel.

Using this website

This website introduces DarkBlaze Group research and development projects. Project descriptions are informational, may change, and should not be treated as a delivery commitment, product guarantee, investment offer, or professional advice.

Project status and materials

Prototypes, demonstrations, and research summaries are shown at different stages of maturity. Do not rely on a prototype or visualization as a production service. Brand assets and project materials remain subject to their respective ownership and license terms; see the third-party notices.

Contact and external apps

Email, WhatsApp, and telephone links are optional handoffs to apps on your device. You choose whether to send a message or place a call. The static website does not send messages, make calls, or promise a response time. Pressing “Agree and continue” explicitly opts you into entering and triggers your browser’s optional location prompt. Location can be denied without blocking entry. If allowed, longitude remains in page memory while this page is open for local solar color adaptation; closing or reloading clears it. The website does not save or transmit longitude. Your browser may remember its own permission setting; the site does not persist consent. Weather is not connected, and location is not used for forecasts; do not rely on the weather indicator for conditions.

Issue reports

If you choose to submit an issue report, DarkBlaze may use the submitted details to reproduce, diagnose, and fix the issue. This site does not collect interaction telemetry or bug reports automatically.

Availability and changes

The website may be updated, interrupted, or withdrawn. Hosting and security arrangements are still subject to production review. These draft terms need owner and jurisdiction-specific legal review before launch.

Contact

Questions may be directed to admin@darkblaze.org using your own email app.
